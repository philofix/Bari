Barii is the Afaan Oromoo word for dawn, the first light of a new day. Our name is our promise: a better start for the people every institution serves.

Our story

Why we started Bari.

We grew up watching essential services run on paper: patients carrying folders between rooms, parents waiting to hear about their children, citizens returning to the same office again and again.

The people running those services were capable and committed. What they lacked were tools built for their work, their languages and their networks.

Bari Technologies was founded in Shashemene to build those tools. We bring together health, product design and engineering, and we work with schools, clinics, operators, businesses and public offices across seven sectors.

The company is focused in Oromia, Ethiopia. We start every project by listening, we build in short steps with our clients, and we stay long after launch.

Vision and mission
Vision

An Ethiopia where every school, clinic, business and public office runs on technology built for the people it serves.

Mission

To design, build and support practical digital systems that make essential services faster, clearer and easier to reach.
